A make.fun entry for "One Emoji, One Dream Vacation": a single emoji that captures one dream trip — no words, just the feeling of being out on the water, far from everything, heading somewhere quiet and beautiful.

Why this one: most dream-vacation answers reach for the same beach, sunrise, or mountain postcard. This entry goes the other way — a slow, human-powered journey through still water, the kind of trip you plan for months and remember for years. It is specific enough to picture instantly, yet open enough that anyone, anywhere, can see their own dream in it: a fjord at dawn, a lake at dusk, a river winding between cliffs. One small boat, one big horizon.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py` and it writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
